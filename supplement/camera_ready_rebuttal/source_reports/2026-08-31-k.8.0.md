training runs and contained one inconclusive G5 row. The product run separates
weak G5 variability from the claim-critical conditional G6 diagnosis and the
corroborating target-stratified S7 sensitivity.

## Product Verdict

```text
composition_shortcut_supported_five_run_robust
```

The result strengthens the central diagnostic while narrowing the weak claim:

```text
Action/value-controlled residual range evidence varies across fresh runs and
is not significant at the between-run level. In contrast, explicit
pre-decision composition is negative in all five runs, and the target-stratified
matched sensitivity agrees, with between-run intervals fully below zero. The
evidence supports a robust composition-shortcut diagnosis through G6, not
ordered belief tracking.
```

Forbidden upgrades remain ordered poker belief tracking, causal hidden-state
mechanism claims, and decision-level pooling as training-run uncertainty.
