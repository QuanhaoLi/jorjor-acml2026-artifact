
# Claim Ledger

## Allowed Claims

1. Positive hidden-state range probes in the audited no-range Limit Hold'em AR model recover nontrivial opponent-range signal under weaker gates.
2. Action/value-controlled residual range signal remains positive in selected seeds but is not sufficient for ordered belief tracking.
3. Conditional G6 is negative in all five fresh runs; S7 is a corroborating post-decision target-stratified sensitivity, not a decision-time matched gate.
4. The submitted three-run audit retains composition-bounded predictive support where its positive prerequisites hold; this is not exact posterior tracking. The fresh five-run panel separately establishes a model-family composition bound and does not assign a new joint CBPS count.
5. The exact-posterior addendum supports a small pointwise increment, while cross-hand pairwise geometry is weak and does not establish temporal posterior tracking.
6. Known-control and oracle rows show that the diagnostic can accept posterior-sensitive states and reject raw composition shortcuts.
7. Validation rows evaluate the diagnostic behavior; they do not upgrade the poker case label.

## Forbidden Claims

1. The poker AR model learns opponent-range belief.
2. Probe accuracy proves a causal mechanism.
3. Synthetic or oracle rows prove that the poker model has belief.
4. CBPS is equivalent to posterior tracking.
5. Projection-level behavior relevance is activation-patching evidence.

## Evidence Boundary

The submitted strict poker verdict remains composition shortcut supported where G1--G5 pass before the composition block. CBPS is a refined interpretation that additionally requires positive behavior prediction. The fresh five-run panel does not rerun all of those prerequisites jointly, so it supports the family-level composition bound rather than a five-run CBPS count. S7 and the support-repaired exact-posterior pair analysis are sensitivities, not substitutes for a decision-time matched gate or temporal-update test.
